PRODUCTION REVIEW

Review job

Generated from the verified scene. Dialogue is quoted only when actually audible; editorial callouts add explanation without pretending to be dialogue.

1. Verify the factual claim

A competitor caption is a lead. Record what the source actually supports and attribute personal testimony.

2. Verify the exact scene

Match the dialogue and visible action to the claim. The payoff must be understandable immediately. Rendering then creates timed dialogue/editorial caption beats and purposeful payoff emphasis.
